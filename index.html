About

Welcome to The Language of Life. This portfolio is a visual journal dedicated to finding the overlooked stories hidden in everyday corners. Through a blend of photography and digital creation, this space explores the subtle moods and unspoken narratives of the world around us.

The two mediums speak different languages. My photography is deeply grounded in memory and physical places. It is intimately tied to reality: real trips, real environments, and tangible moments in time. My generative art, conversely, is driven by logic and iteration through creative coding. Just as a woven textile uses a strict grid to create something soft and tactile, I use precise code to generate patterns that feel alive.

Through both the lens and the code, photography and abstraction keep circling back to one another in a continuous and unspoken dialogue. The camera captures the fleeting reality of a physical space, while the algorithm builds an intricate framework to explore the memory further.
